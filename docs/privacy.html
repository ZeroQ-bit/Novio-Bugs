Vortexo Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 3, 2026

Vortexo is a media client for Apple TV. The app itself does not provide bundled media content. Users may choose to connect Plex, Live TV/IPTV, Nuvio, or compatible user-provided sources.

Data We May Process

  • App settings such as preferences, subtitle settings, and profile configuration.
  • Local playback data such as watch progress, continue watching, watchlist, and viewing state.
  • Source configuration data entered by the user.
  • Diagnostic information and crash data provided by Apple or TestFlight.

How Data Is Used

  • To provide playback features and save your preferences.
  • To restore watch progress and improve the user experience.
  • To connect to sources and services selected by the user.
  • To diagnose app stability and performance issues.

Third-Party Sources and Services

If you add third-party sources or services, requests are made to those services as part of the app’s functionality. Those third parties operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

Vortexo does not sell personal data. Data is only shared with third-party services when required to use sources or services you choose to connect.

Data Retention

Locally stored app data remains on your device unless you remove the app, clear app data, or remove connected sources.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the official Vortexo support page: https://vortexo.app/support.html.
